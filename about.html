About MozTools

Welcome to MozTools! We are a free, all-in-one file conversion platform dedicated to making your digital life easier.

Our Mission

We believe that converting files should be fast, simple, and secure. Whether you need to convert a PDF to Word for an important project, shrink a large image for an email, or turn a photo into a PDF, MozTools is here to help you do it in seconds.

Privacy & Security

Your privacy is our top priority. Our image compressor runs entirely inside your web browser, meaning your photos never leave your device! For document conversions, we use secure, industry-standard APIs that automatically delete your files after the conversion is complete.

Why Choose Us?

Our tools are 100% free, require no registration, and work perfectly on both mobile phones and desktop computers. We are constantly adding new tools to help you get things done.
