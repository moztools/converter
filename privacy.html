Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At MozTools, accessible from our website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by MozTools and how we use it.

1. File Processing

Our file conversion tools process your files securely using third-party APIs (CloudConvert). We do not permanently store, keep, or sell your uploaded files. Files are processed for the conversion job you request and are removed shortly after processing is complete.

2. Log Files

MozTools follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

3. Google AdSense and Cookies

Like any other website, MozTools uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

Google is one of a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet. However, visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at the following URL: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. Third Party Privacy Policies

MozTools's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we are advising you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information. It may include their practices and instructions about how to opt-out of certain options.

5. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its Terms and Conditions.
